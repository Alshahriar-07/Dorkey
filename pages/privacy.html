Privacy first

Privacy policy.

Dorkey is designed to work without collecting personal information.

Data handling

Dorkey does not require an account and does not collect names, email addresses, typing histories, analytics, advertising identifiers, or payment information.

Browser storage

Your latest test result is saved in sessionStorage so the result page can display it during your browser session. A small consent preference may be saved in localStorage. You can clear both through your browser settings.

Cookies

Dorkey does not set tracking or advertising cookies. The consent prompt refers to browser storage used for the local preference described above.

External links

Links to Eagox Studio and the creator open external websites. Their privacy practices are separate from Dorkey.

Updates

If the way Dorkey handles information changes, this page will be updated to describe the change clearly.
